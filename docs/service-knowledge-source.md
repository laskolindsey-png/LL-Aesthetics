## BBL HERO® (BroadBand Light)

**What It Is** — BBL HERO® (High Energy Rapid Output) is an advanced broadband
light treatment that uses pulses of light to improve skin tone, reduce sun
damage, minimize redness, treat age spots, and stimulate collagen production.
Commonly used on the face, neck, chest, arms, legs, and hands.

**Good candidate** — sun damage / age spots; uneven skin tone; freckles; facial
redness or rosacea; small visible blood vessels; dull skin; mild acne; those
wanting healthier, younger-looking skin with minimal downtime.

**Not a good candidate** — pregnant/breastfeeding (unless physician-approved);
active skin infection; open wounds; recent tanning or sunburn; photosensitizing
medications; very dark or recently tanned skin (may require evaluation); history
of certain light-sensitive disorders.

**Benefits** — brightens skin; improves tone; reduces pigmentation; stimulates
collagen; improves texture; reduces redness; treats broken capillaries; little to
no downtime; quick sessions.

**Pre-Care** — avoid sun 2 weeks before; SPF 30+ daily; no self-tanners for 2
weeks; stop retinoids/exfoliants 5–7 days prior; no waxing or chemical peels in
the area for 1 week; arrive with clean skin (no makeup/lotion/sunscreen); inform
provider of medications/recent procedures.

**During** — 15–45 minutes depending on area; protective eyewear worn; sensation
like a warm rubber-band snap; cooling technology for comfort.

**Post-Care** — sunscreen daily; avoid direct sun 2 weeks; no hot tubs/saunas/
vigorous exercise for 24 hrs; gentle skincare for several days; pigmented spots
may darken then flake away; keep moisturized; don't pick or scratch.

**Possible side effects** — mild redness; warm sensation; temporary swelling;
darkening of pigmented lesions; mild peeling; rare blistering or pigment changes.

**When to call the office** — severe blistering; increasing pain; signs of
infection; significant swelling >72 hrs; fever; drainage or pus.

**Frequency** — initial series 3–5 treatments, 4–6 weeks apart; maintenance every
6–12 months.

## BOTOX®

**What It Is** — FDA-approved injectable neuromodulator that temporarily relaxes
facial muscles responsible for dynamic wrinkles; softens expression lines and
helps prevent deeper wrinkles.

**Good candidate** — adults wanting to reduce facial wrinkles; forehead lines;
frown lines ("11s"); crow's feet; bunny lines; lip flip; chin dimpling; neck
bands; preventative treatment.

**Not a good candidate** — pregnant/breastfeeding; active infection at injection
sites; neuromuscular disorders (e.g., Myasthenia Gravis); allergy to botulinum
toxin ingredients; certain neurological conditions.

**Benefits** — smooths wrinkles; prevents progression; quick; no downtime;
natural-looking results when expertly injected; lasts ~3–4 months.

**Pre-Care** — avoid alcohol 24 hrs; avoid aspirin/ibuprofen/fish oil/vitamin E
and other blood thinners for several days (only if provider-approved); clean
skin; inform provider of medications and prior Botox.

**During** — tiny injections with a fine needle; ~10–20 minutes; minimal
discomfort.

**Post-Care** — remain upright 4 hrs; no rubbing/massaging treated areas 24 hrs;
no strenuous exercise until next day; no facials 24 hrs; gently move facial
muscles the first hour if instructed; results begin 3–5 days, fully develop ~2
weeks.

**Possible side effects** — mild redness; swelling; bruising; temporary headache;
tenderness; rare eyelid or eyebrow drooping.

**When to call the office** — difficulty swallowing; difficulty breathing; vision
changes; severe weakness; allergic reaction; persistent eyelid drooping.

**Frequency** — every 3–4 months for consistent wrinkle prevention.

## CHEMICAL PEELS

**What It Is** — specially formulated acids exfoliate damaged outer skin to reveal
smoother, healthier, brighter skin; strength varies by skin goals.

**Good candidate** — acne-prone skin; acne scars; fine lines; sun damage; uneven
pigmentation; melasma (selected cases); rough texture; enlarged pores.

**Not a good candidate** — pregnancy (certain peels); active cold sores; active
skin infection; recent Accutane (6–12 months depending on peel); open wounds;
severe eczema flare; recent excessive sun exposure.

**Benefits** — brighter skin; improved texture; reduced acne; softer fine lines;
improved pigmentation; collagen stimulation; healthier complexion.

**Pre-Care** — stop retinoids 5–7 days before; no waxing/hair removal for a week;
sunscreen daily; no tanning; notify provider of cold-sore history.

**During** — solution applied for several minutes; mild tingling/warmth/stinging
common; ~30–45 minutes.

**Post-Care** — peeling for several days depending on depth; don't pick peeling
skin; moisturize frequently; SPF 30–50 daily; avoid exfoliants until healed;
avoid excessive heat/sweating 24–48 hrs.

**Possible side effects** — redness; peeling; tightness; dryness; temporary
sensitivity; rare pigment changes.

**When to call the office** — severe swelling; blistering; signs of infection;
fever; severe pain; yellow drainage.

**Frequency** — every 4–6 weeks for a series of 3–6, then maintenance every few
months depending on goals.

## DERMAL FILLER

**What It Is** — injectable gels (most commonly hyaluronic acid) used to restore
lost volume, smooth wrinkles, enhance contours, and improve facial balance. Areas
include lips, cheeks, chin, jawline, temples, and under-eye.

**Good candidate** — volume loss from aging; thin lips; deep smile lines;
marionette lines; chin enhancement; jawline contouring; cheek enhancement; facial
balancing; non-surgical rejuvenation.

**Not a good candidate** — pregnant/breastfeeding; active skin infection; active
cold sores near the area; severe allergies to filler ingredients; certain
autoimmune conditions (provider evaluation); unrealistic expectations.

**Benefits** — immediate improvement; restores volume; enhances symmetry; softens
wrinkles; natural-looking; minimal downtime; long-lasting (typically 6–18 months
depending on product and area).

**Pre-Care** — avoid alcohol 24 hrs; avoid aspirin/ibuprofen/fish oil/vitamin E
and blood thinners for several days if provider-approved; stay hydrated; inform
provider of cold-sore history; avoid dental work ~2 weeks before and after when
possible.

**During** — provider assesses anatomy and goals, then places filler with needle
or cannula; numbing cream or lidocaine-containing filler for comfort; ~30–60
minutes.

**Post-Care** — ice as directed; avoid strenuous exercise 24–48 hrs; avoid alcohol
24 hrs; sleep with head elevated the first night; avoid touching/massaging unless
instructed; bruising and swelling expected, improving within several days.

**Possible side effects** — swelling; bruising; tenderness; redness; small lumps;
itching; temporary asymmetry while swelling resolves. Rare but serious: infection
or blockage of a blood vessel — requires immediate medical attention.

**When to call the office** — severe pain; skin turning pale/white/purple/dark;
blistering; vision changes; fever; increasing redness or warmth; drainage;
significant swelling that worsens after several days.

**Frequency** — most fillers last 6–18 months; maintenance individualized by
product, area, and goals.

## FOREVER CLEAR BBL®
Broadband light designed for acne — targets acne bacteria, reduces inflammation,
promotes healing; improves post-acne redness. Good for mild–moderate/inflammatory
acne, oily skin, medication-free option. Avoid: recent tan/sunburn,
photosensitizing meds/conditions, pregnancy (consult), active infection.
Pre: no tanning 2 wks, SPF, stop retinoids if instructed, clean skin. Post: gentle
skincare, SPF, don't pick, follow acne regimen. **Call:** blistering, severe
swelling, infection, persistent pain, fever. **Frequency:** every 2–4 wks ×4–6,
then maintenance.

## HALO® LASER RESURFACING
World's first hybrid fractional laser (ablative + non-ablative) for texture,
pigment, fine lines, pores, acne scars — less downtime than traditional. Numbing
applied; 60–90 min; sunburn-like redness + bronzing as skin exfoliates. Avoid:
infection, open wounds, recent tan, pregnancy (consult), meds affecting healing,
can't avoid sun. Pre: no sun 2 wks, SPF 30–50, stop retinoids/exfoliants 5–7 days,
hydrate, antiviral if cold-sore history. Post: clean+moisturize, no sun until
healed, don't pick, sleep elevated, no exercise/hot tubs/saunas 48 hrs. **Call:**
fever, increasing pain after first days, pus/foul drainage, severe swelling,
blistering, infection. **Frequency:** 1–2 treatments, maintenance every 1–2 yrs.

## HYDRAFACIAL®
Non-invasive cleanse + exfoliate + painless extractions + hydrating serums; no
downtime. Good for nearly all skin (dry, oily, congested, dull, sensitive). Avoid:
active cold sores, open wounds, severe sunburn, infection, ingredient allergy,
severe rosacea flare. Pre: no retinoids 2–3 days, no waxing/peels 1 wk, clean
skin. Post: SPF, no heavy sweat 24 hrs, no exfoliants 48 hrs, hydrate; makeup next
day. **Call:** redness >48 hrs, blistering, severe swelling, infection, reaction.
**Frequency:** every 4–6 weeks.

## LASER HAIR REMOVAL
Light energy targets follicles; hair returns finer/lighter, eventually reduced.
Best on dark hair. Avoid: pregnancy (consult), infection, open wounds, recent
tan/sunburn, photosensitizing meds; white/gray/red hair less effective. Pre: shave
24 hrs before, no wax/tweeze/thread 4–6 wks, no tan 2 wks, SPF. Post: cool
compress, no hot showers/saunas/exercise 24–48 hrs, no sun, SPF 30+, keep shaving,
no waxing between. **Call:** blistering, severe pain, infection, large swelling,
worsening discoloration. **Frequency:** every 4–8 wks, 6–8 treatments, maintenance
1–2×/yr.

## MICRONEEDLING
Sterile micro-needles create controlled micro-injuries → collagen/elastin for
texture, scars, pores, fine lines. Numbing applied; 45–60 min. Avoid: active
acne/infection/cold sores, open wounds, pregnancy (consult), bleeding disorders,
recent Accutane. Pre: stop retinoids 5–7 days, no sun, hydrate, no alcohol 24 hrs.
Post: sunburn-like redness 24–48 hrs, no makeup 24 hrs, no exercise/sauna/swim
24–48 hrs, gentle products, SPF, no retinoids/acids ~1 wk. **Call:** redness
increasing after 72 hrs, fever, drainage, severe swelling, infection, reaction.
**Frequency:** 3–6 treatments 4–6 wks apart, maintenance every 6–12 mo.

## PRF EZ GEL
Natural injectable from patient's own blood; PRF processed into gel — subtle
volume + growth factors for collagen. Areas: under-eyes, cheeks, around mouth.
Avoid: infection, clotting/platelet disorders, severe anemia, certain
anticoagulants (evaluation), unrealistic expectations. Pre: hydrate 24–48 hrs,
good meal, no alcohol 24 hrs, discuss blood thinners. Post: ice, no exercise 24
hrs, no heat/hot tubs/saunas 24–48 hrs, don't massage. **Call:** severe pain,
increasing redness/warmth, fever, drainage, infection, worsening swelling.
**Frequency:** 2–3 treatments 6–8 wks apart, maintenance every 6–12 mo.

## PRF HAIR RESTORATION
PRF growth factors injected into scalp to stimulate dormant follicles / thicker
hair; early thinning in men & women. Avoid: scalp infection, bleeding/platelet
disorders, severe anemia, chemo, fully bald areas, unrealistic expectations. Pre:
hydrate 24–48 hrs, good meal, no alcohol 24 hrs, wash hair AM, no heavy product,
discuss blood thinners. Post: no hair wash 12–24 hrs, no exercise 24 hrs, no
swim/hot tub/sauna 24–48 hrs, no color/chemical ~1 wk. **Call:** fever, increasing
pain, pus/drainage, severe swelling, infection, reaction. **Frequency:** 3–4
treatments 4–6 wks apart, maintenance every 6–12 mo.

## PRX-T33®
Non-peeling biorevitalization (TCA + hydrogen peroxide + kojic acid) — collagen,
firmness, texture, pigment with minimal/no peeling. Face, neck, chest, body.
Avoid: infection, open wounds, active eczema/dermatitis, ingredient allergy,
pregnancy/breastfeeding (discretion), severe sunburn. Pre: no retinoids 5–7 days,
no wax/aggressive exfoliation 1 wk, no excess sun, SPF, clean skin. Post:
moisturize, SPF 30–50, no excess heat 24 hrs, no exfoliants ~1 wk. **Call:** severe
swelling, blistering, increasing pain, fever, drainage, infection. **Frequency:**
4–6 treatments every 1–2 wks, maintenance every 3–6 mo.

## XERF® SKIN TIGHTENING
Non-invasive radiofrequency — controlled heat → collagen/elastin for firmness and
mild–moderate laxity; no downtime; face & body. Avoid: pregnancy, implanted
electronic devices (e.g., pacemaker) unless cleared, infection, open wounds, metal
implants in area (evaluation), severe laxity needing surgery. Pre: hydrate, no
excess sun, clean skin, remove jewelry, disclose implants/devices. Post: resume
normal activity, moisturize, SPF, hydrate. **Call:** severe pain, blistering,
increasing swelling after 48 hrs, fever, infection, unexpected skin changes.
**Frequency:** 4–6 treatments every 2–4 wks, maintenance every 6–12 mo.

## BOTOX vs JEUVEAU — provider reference (NOT for Chloe / not patient-facing)

Both are injectable botulinum toxin type A neuromodulators that temporarily reduce
muscle movement causing expression lines. Key differences: branding, FDA-approved
uses, formulation, pricing/rewards programs, and individual response.

- **Botox Cosmetic** — onabotulinumtoxinA; Allergan/AbbVie; broader FDA cosmetic
  labeling (frown lines, crow's feet, forehead lines, platysma bands); longer
  history; multiple medical uses; rewards = Allē; often higher price.
- **Jeuveau** — prabotulinumtoxinA-xvfs; Evolus; FDA approved for glabellar ("11")
  lines; approved US 2019; aesthetics-focused; rewards = Evolus Rewards; often
  more affordable.
- **Units are NOT interchangeable** between products; potency units can't be
  directly compared/converted (per both FDA labels).
- **Onset/longevity:** begin within several days, fuller at ~2 weeks, last ~3–4
  months; maintenance required. Jeuveau's maker notes onset as early as 2 days.
- **Boxed warning:** rare possibility of toxin effects spreading beyond the
  injection site; treat only under an appropriately licensed/trained professional.
- **LL Aesthetics pricing:** Jeuveau $12/unit, Botox $12.50/unit.
